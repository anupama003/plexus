Meet Fernwave.
An EU fintech with a lot to trace.

Fernwave B.V. is a fictional payments company in Amsterdam. It sells card terminals and a desktop treasury app to businesses across Europe, and runs identity, open-banking and settlement services behind them. That mix puts it under both EU laws: the Cyber Resilience Act for what it sells, and DORA for the financial services it runs.
